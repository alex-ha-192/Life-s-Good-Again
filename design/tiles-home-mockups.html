Four directions for Tiles Home, drawn at TV size

Each screen is a 1920×1080 frame of your 43-inch TV, scaled down. Click a tile to move the focus ring and see how the selected state looks. Sample apps are shown as names. On the TV, Tiles Home uses each app's real icon.

Fixed in every direction
  • Safe margins of 110 px, so nothing is lost to overscan
  • One focus style: white ring, slight lift, no wobble
  • Real app icons in place of letters (a "Y" for YouTube)
  • The clock shows date and weekday, not only time
  • A colour-key hint bar that matches what the keys do

How to answer

Reply with a letter, or mix them, and add anything to change. For example: B, but with A's single colour or C without the Dev Mode tile. I'll build the one you pick into the launcher and rebuild the .ipk.

The status tiles in C (privacy settings, Developer Mode days) only work if the TV lets an app read those values. I'd check that on your firmware first. If it doesn't, the launcher would show them from the last time lgtv_privacy.py ran.
