Layout C, refined: a ring of five pages with icons and titles only

Paging wraps around. The page in the middle is the one you're on, and the two pages each side shrink and fade, so the whole menu is always in view. Click the screen and use your arrow keys, or the buttons.

Favourites

On any page, press yellow on an app, input or setting to add it to favourites. It stays where it was and gets a small star there. Press yellow again to take it out.

On the favourites page, yellow removes it from favourites only. Red picks it up: move it with ↑ ↓, then press OK or red to put it down. Keyboard: Y and R.

Icons

On the TV, every row uses the app's own icon, taken from the TV by export-tiles. Here they're stand-in glyphs on each app's colour, because the mockup can't show the real logos. Inputs use the TV's own HDMI, antenna and USB icons.

Font

Segoe UI is Microsoft's and can't be shipped inside the app. Your computer shows it here if it's installed. On the TV the launcher will bundle Open Sans, which has an open licence and the same designer as Segoe UI, so it looks very close. Selawik, Microsoft's open Segoe stand-in, is an option too.
